About Dadar Spa

Discover how we created a peaceful wellness retreat right in the bustling heart of Dadar East, Mumbai.

Our Philosophy

Where Ancient Wisdom Meets Contemporary Luxury

At Dadar Spa, located at Dastoorwadi, Dadar East, we believe relaxation is not a luxury, but a fundamental necessity for living a healthy, balanced life in Mumbai.

From the moment you step into our calm, fragrant corridors, the noise and frenzy of Mumbai fades away. Our thoughtfully crafted therapies blend time-honored Ayurvedic principles, authentic Thai pressure-point methods, and soothing European Swedish strokes with 100% natural cold-pressed essential oils.

Mindful Healing

Calms nervous system and restores circadian balance.

Pure Botanicals

Unrefined, additive-free aromatherapeutic herbal oils.
