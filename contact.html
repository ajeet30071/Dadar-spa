Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an advance appointment or can I walk in?

While walk-ins are welcome, we strongly recommend booking in advance via phone or WhatsApp at 8169595455 to guarantee therapist availability and avoid waiting time.

We maintain the highest hygiene standards with disposable single-use sheets, freshly laundered towels, sanitized shower cabins, and organic, skin-tested oils for every client.

Are shower and steam facilities included in treatments?

Yes! Every full-body massage therapy includes complimentary access to our private hot shower suites and steam bath facilities with fresh organic shower toiletries.
